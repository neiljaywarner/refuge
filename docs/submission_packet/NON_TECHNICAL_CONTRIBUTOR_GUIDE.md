## 👋 Welcome! No Technical Experience Needed
You do **not** need to know how to code, use the terminal, or understand Serverpod to make a huge impact on our hackathon submission. 

Judges and potential partners care deeply about **how the product feels to real humans**, how clearly the mission is communicated, and how accessible it is to people experiencing digital burnout. Here are four high-impact ways you can dive in and help right now!

---

## 🌟 4 Ways You Can Jump In Right Now

### 1. 📱 Test the Live Web App on Your Phone or Computer (5 Minutes)
- **Open this link**: [https://ministry-demos--sanctuary-cnf3fnh7.web.app](https://ministry-demos--sanctuary-cnf3fnh7.web.app)
- **Things to try**:
  1. Switch between the 4 themes (Sage, Clean Slate, Monastic Dark, High Contrast) using the dropdown at the top right. Which one feels most peaceful to your eyes?
  2. Tap between the Fellowship Spaces: *Main Hall*, *Quiet Room*, *Prayer & Care*, and *Gratitude Journal*.
  3. Try posting a short reflection and tagging it with an intent (e.g. *Giving Thanks* or *Quiet Reflection*).
  4. Tap the qualitative reaction buttons (*🙏 Praying*, *🤍 Standing With*, *🕊️ Peace*, *🌱 Encouraged*).
- **Tell us**: Did anything feel confusing? Did you feel a sense of calm compared to social media?

### 3. ✍️ Review the Submission Story & Copy (10 Minutes)
- Open [`docs/submission_packet/DEVPOST_SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md`](file:///Users/neil/AndroidStudioProjects/refuge_13oct_2026_prototype_for_serverpod_hackathon/docs/submission_packet/DEVPOST_SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md).
