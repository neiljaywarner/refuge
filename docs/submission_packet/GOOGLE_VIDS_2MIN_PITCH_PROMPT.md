# 🎬 Google Vids 2-Minute Hackathon Pitch Prompt & Storyboard
**Project**: Refuge (Sanctuary) — Calm-Tech Fellowship & Safe Haven  
**Target Platform**: Google Vids (Google Workspace)  
**Target Duration**: 2 Minutes (120 seconds)  
**Tone**: Warm, authoritative, innovative, calm, and inspiring  

---

## 📋 Ready-to-Paste Prompt for Google Vids

```text
Create a compelling, professional 2-minute hackathon pitch video about "Refuge" — an unhurried, zero-badge, sensory-friendly community platform built with Flutter and Serverpod 4 for the neurodivergent Christian community (Belong on the Spectrum).

Video Style & Mood:
- Modern, clean, calm-tech visual design using sage green, warm neutrals, and crisp dark modes.
- Unhurried pacing with smooth transitions (no frantic flashing graphics or strobe effects).
- Warm, articulate voiceover narration paired with gentle, grounding acoustic ambient music.
- Integrated dual-smartphone mockup frames showcasing real physical Google Pixel 9a devices communicating live over T-Mobile LTE cellular data.

Structure (120 seconds total):
1. [0:00 - 0:20] The Problem: The crisis of notification exhaustion, red badge anxiety, and sensory overload in modern communication apps.
2. [0:20 - 0:45] The Solution: Introducing Refuge — zero badges, 4 WCAG AAA sensory modes, curated calm-tech memes, and qualitative care affirmations.
3. [0:45 - 1:15] Live Cellular Demo: Side-by-side Pixel 9a hardware screen recordings showing real-time reflection exchange and live prayer affirmations via Serverpod Cloud WebSockets.
4. [1:15 - 1:40] Full-Stack Architecture: Unified Dart ecosystem, CodeWithAndrea Riverpod feature-first client architecture, Serverpod 4 SPI compilation, PostgreSQL 16 persistence, and 26/26 automated tests.
5. [1:40 - 2:00] Real-World Impact: Cutting $1,500/year SaaS costs down to <$5/month compute, live web deployment on Firebase CDN, and invitation for judges to test the in-app API Explorer.
```

---

## ⏱️ Detailed 2-Minute Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: The Urgent World vs. The Need for Peace (0:00 – 0:20)
* **Visuals**:
  * Montage of cluttered smartphone home screens overflowing with red notification badges (`(99+)`, `(1,420)`), vibration ripples, and flashing banner alerts.
  * Contrast cuts to a serene, natural sage botanical gradient with typography: *"What if communication software protected your peace instead of exploiting your attention?"*
* **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Every major community app today is weaponized for urgency. Red badge counters, unread pings, and dopamine loops induce chronic sensory overload—especially for neurodivergent individuals and mindful fellowship groups. We set out to change that."*

---

### Scene 2: Introducing Refuge (0:20 – 0:45)
* **Visuals**:
  * Hero showcase of Refuge running smoothly across desktop browser and mobile.
  * Highlight callouts zooming into:
    1. **Zero-Badge Navigation**: Clean space selector with zero unread badges.
    2. **4 WCAG AAA Sensory Themes**: Sage Botanical, Clean Slate, Monastic Dark, and High Contrast.
    3. **Curated Calm-Tech Memes**: Relatable neurodivergent Christian humor without strobe GIFs or trackers.
    4. **Qualitative Care Affirmations**: Replacing vanity "Likes" with *🙏 Praying*, *🤍 Support*, and *🌱 Encouraged*.
* **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Meet Refuge: an unhurried, zero-badge safe haven built with Flutter and Serverpod 4. In partnership with Belong on the Spectrum, Refuge eliminates every notification counter, introduces 4 WCAG AAA sensory themes, and replaces vanity algorithms with authentic care affirmations."*

---

### Scene 3: Live Dual-Pixel Cellular Demonstration (0:45 – 1:15)
* **Visuals**:
  * Split-screen view featuring **Pixel Right** (left side of screen) and **Pixel Left** (right side of screen).
  * Video clips from `sanctuary_pixel_right_staggered.mp4` and `sanctuary_pixel_left_staggered.mp4`.
  * **Timestamp 0:50**: Pixel Right types a reflection, closes the keyboard, and taps *Publish Reflection*.
  * **Timestamp 0:58**: Sub-100ms later, Pixel Left receives the post live via Serverpod Cloud WebSockets!
  * **Timestamp 1:04**: Pixel Left taps *🙏 Praying*, instantly updating the counter on both screens in real time.
  * **Timestamp 1:10**: Pixel Right switches to *Monastic Dark* theme via the top-right popup menu.
* **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Here it is running live on two physical Google Pixel 9a devices over real T-Mobile LTE cellular data. As a member publishes an unhurried reflection on the right, it syncs instantly to the phone on the left through Serverpod Cloud WebSockets. No polling, no latency, and zero artificial urgency."*

---

### Scene 4: Full-Stack Dart Engineering & Craftsmanship (1:15 – 1:40)
* **Visuals**:
  * Architectural schematic diagram showing unified Dart flowing from client to cloud:
    * **Frontend**: CodeWithAndrea Riverpod feature-first architecture (`features/reflections/`, `features/sensory_themes/`, `features/memes/`).
    * **Backend**: Serverpod 4 SPI protocol models, WebSocket streaming endpoints, PostgreSQL 16 database, and Redis 7 cache.
    * **Testing Badge**: `26 / 26 Tests Passing (100% Pass Rate) • 0 Analyzer Warnings`.
    * **Resilience Moat**: 800ms offline fallback guarantee.
* **Voiceover Narration**:
  > *"Under the hood, Refuge showcases principal-grade full-stack Dart. We migrated to CodeWithAndrea's Riverpod feature-first architecture, paired with Serverpod 4 SPI models that guarantee end-to-end type safety with zero drift. Backed by PostgreSQL 16, Redis 7, an 800ms offline fallback moat, and 26 comprehensive automated tests."*
