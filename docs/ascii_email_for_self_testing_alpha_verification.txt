3. STEP-BY-STEP SELF-TESTING CHECKLIST (BEFORE ASKING OTHERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow these 8 quick steps on your phone or computer:

[ ] STEP 1: OPEN LIVE WEB APP
    - Navigate to: https://belongonthespectrum.web.app
    - Confirm the calm cream background and welcome header load smoothly.

[ ] STEP 2: TEST PWA INSTALLATION
    - On Android: Chrome 3-dots menu -> "Install app".
    - On iPhone: Safari Share button -> "Add to Home Screen".
    - Launch from your home screen and confirm it opens full-screen without URL bars.

[ ] STEP 3: TEST REGISTRATION (SIGN UP)
    - Tap profile icon in the top right -> tap "Sign In / Register".
    - Switch to the "Register" tab.
    - Enter a name, email, and password.
    - Select your ICOC church affiliation from the dropdown.
    - Tap "Create Account" and verify instant profile update.

[ ] STEP 4: TEST SIGN OUT & SIGN IN (LOGIN)
    - Open profile menu -> tap "Sign Out".
    - Tap "Sign In", enter your credentials, and verify you are signed back in.

[ ] STEP 5: TEST THREADED DISCUSSION
    - On any reflection card, tap the discussion bubble icon.
    - Read existing replies and tap "Reply" to post a nested reply.
    - Tap a care reaction (`🙏` Praying or `🌱` Growing).

[ ] STEP 6: TEST SENSORY SOUNDSCAPES
    - Tap the music/sound icon in the bottom bar or spaces.
    - Tap "Warm Brown Noise" -> verify playback starts calmly.
    - Select the 15-minute sleep timer chip.

[ ] STEP 7: TEST THEME SWITCHER
    - Tap the sensory theme selector.
    - Toggle between Sage, Clean, Dark, and Contrast.
    - Confirm all text remains legible and unclipped.

[ ] STEP 8: TRIGGER 7-TAP EASTER EGG
    - Rapidly tap the "Belong on the Spectrum" top title 7 times.
    - Verify the Observability Dialog opens displaying session logs.
    - Tap "Simulate Handled Error (NFE)" to verify AppLogger error capture.
