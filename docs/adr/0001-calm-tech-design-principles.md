# ADR 0001: Calm-Tech Design Principles

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Traditional social applications employ dopamine-driven psychological hooks, including unread notification badges, typing indicators, active presence indicators, and algorithmic feed sorting designed to maximize time-on-app and induce urgency.

For a fellowship and spiritual reflection clubhouse ("Sanctuary"), these design patterns induce cognitive fatigue, distraction, and anxiety, conflicting directly with contemplative, restful community engagement.

## Decision
We enforce a strict Calm-Tech standard across the entire application:
1. **Zero Badges**: No red numerical badges, unread dot indicators, or alert counts anywhere in the UI.
2. **Zero Urgency Indicators**: No typing bubbles ("... is typing"), active online indicators, or read receipts.
3. **Unhurried Asynchronous Communication**: Chronological reflection feed without algorithmic outrage sorting.
4. **Intent Tags**: Compulsory intent categorization (`[Prayer Request]`, `[Comfort Only]`, `[Question]`, `[Resource]`) to frame emotional context before a post is read.
5. **Qualitative Care Reactions**: Replacing binary or generic engagement metrics ("Likes") with intentional affirmations (`🙏 Praying`, `🤍 Support`).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Peaceful, non-addictive user experience; accessible to users experiencing sensory fatigue.
- **Trade-off**: Lower daily session frequency compared to dopamine-optimized apps, which is intentional and aligns with our core mission.
