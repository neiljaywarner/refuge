## ✨ What Makes Belong on the Spectrum Special?

1. **🌿 4 WCAG AAA Sensory Themes**
   - Choose between **Clean Slate**, **Sage Botanical**, **Monastic Dark**, and **High Contrast Charcoal** to match your sensory and visual comfort.
2. **📖 Grounded in Scripture**
   - Anchored in Psalm 139:14 — *"I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well."*
3. **🕊️ Calm Notifications (No Overwhelm)**
   - No intrusive red badges or endless notification spam. We practice calm-tech with digest summaries, quiet hours, and gentle haptics.
4. **💬 Themed Fellowship Spaces**
   - Focused, thoughtful spaces (`General Fellowship`, `Scripture & Reflections`, `Creative Expressions`, `Quiet Room`) with threaded conversations and care reactions (`🙏`, `🤍`, `🌱`, `😄`).
5. **🛡️ Safe, Private & Non-Profit**
   - 100% free forever, open source, zero commercial ads, and zero third-party tracking pixels.

## 📱 How to Join the Exploration

1. **Open the Live Web Sanctuary**:  
   Visit [https://belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://belongonthespectrum.web.app) on your phone, tablet, or laptop.
2. **Install to Your Home Screen (Optional)**:  
   - **Android Chrome**: Tap the three-dot menu `⋮` and select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.  
   - **iPhone Safari**: Tap the Share button `⎋` and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.  
   Enjoy a full-screen, native application experience!
3. **Sign In or Register**:  
   Sign in with your email. Select your church community fellowship or indicate your independent participation.
4. **Try the Sensory Switcher**:  
   Tap the sensory theme icon in the app bar to find the color scheme that feels best on your eyes.
5. **Share a Greeting**:  
   Leave a reflection or reply in the `General Fellowship` space!
