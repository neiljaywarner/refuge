# A note for Carina: Refuge for our Bema group

*From Neil · November 2026 · Still early: a work in progress, not a finished product.*

## First, a question for you

Before I tell you anything about Refuge, I'd love to hear from you: what works well for your group on Discord today? I want to build on what you already love, not change things for the sake of it.

## Why I'm building this

I'd love for your Bema group to have a calm home made for what you do each week: listen, gather, talk, and pray for each other. Refuge is the app I've been building for communities like that, and I keep picturing your group in it. If Discord turns out to be the better fit, that's a real answer, and I'd love to know why.

## What works today

* Group rooms such as Prayer Sanctuary, Weekly Study, and Online Resources / Videos.  
* Posts, plus a "praying" reaction so people know they're being prayed for.  
* A calm, accessible design, with large text and gentle themes.

## Almost there (in a test version)

* A video call with screen share that guests can join from a link.  
* Comments and replies on posts.

Both are working in my test version but aren't reliable enough yet, so I'd rather show them to you once they are.

## Still to come

* Your own group, set up with the channel names you already use.  
* A steady weekly call link, so everyone knows where to go each week.  
* Ideas I'd love your thoughts on: gentle reminders to listen to the episode, and a simple way to see the week's episodes at a glance.

## How you could help

* Which channels and features your group actually uses on Discord.  
* What your members are on: iPhone or Android.  
* Which episode the group is on right now.  
* Whether you'd try a short guided demo when it's ready.

## Questions I'd love to ask you

1. What's the most frustrating part of running the group on Discord?  
2. Which channels would you keep, and which could go?  
3. What would make it easier for people to keep up with the episodes each week?  
4. Would your group welcome reminders, and if so, by text, email, or in the app?
