# Refuge for Our Bema Group – Talking Points

*For Carina, from Neil · November 2026 · Still early — this is a work in progress, not a finished product.*

## Why I'm building this

I'd love for your Bema group to have a calmer home than Discord — a place made for exactly what you do each week: listen, gather, talk, and pray for each other. Refuge is the app I've been building for communities like that, and I keep picturing your group in it.

## What already works

- **Group spaces**, like rooms for prayer requests, videos, announcements, and general chat.  
- **Posts and comments**, so people can share and reply in each room.  
- **Reactions**, including a "praying" reaction for prayer requests.  
- **A video call with screen share**, and guests can join from a simple link.

## What's still coming

- **Your own group**, set up with the channel names you already use.  
- **A steady weekly call link**, so everyone knows exactly where to go each week.  
- **Ideas I'd love your thoughts on:** gentle reminders to listen to the episode, and a simple way to see the week's episodes at a glance (and whether to split them up).

## How you could help

- Tell me what you like and dislike about Discord today.  
- Tell me which channels your group actually uses.  
- When it's ready, would you be up for a short demo?

## Questions I'd love to ask you

1. What's the most frustrating part of running the group on Discord?  
2. Which channels would you keep, and which could go?  
3. What would make it easier for people to keep up with the episodes each week?  
4. Would your group welcome reminders — and if so, by text, email, or in the app?
