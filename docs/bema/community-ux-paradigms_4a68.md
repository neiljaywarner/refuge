# Refuge – Community UX Paradigms: Discord, Circle-Style Spaces, and Others

*Review draft for an AI reviewer (Gemini, Neon, or Argon) · Sat Nov 21, 2026 · Purpose: describe how the main community-app paradigms differ, and where Refuge might benefit from borrowing from each. Items marked **Open question** need a decision. This is analysis, not a decision.*

## 1\. Why this doc

Neil's instinct is that Refuge should already be close to replacing Carina's Discord for her Bema group, since both are community apps and the GetStream call mostly works. That's largely right. The open question is **which interaction model fits a weekly Bema discussion group best**, and whether Refuge should move toward Discord, stay Circle-style, or borrow from other models.

## 2\. Where Refuge is today

Refuge is the platform; Belong on the Spectrum is its first live community. It is **Circle-style**: a Feed, Spaces (topic rooms such as Start Here, Announcements, General Chat, Online Resources / Videos, and Prayer Sanctuary), posts with comments, reactions (including praying/relate), Giphy, DMs, a member directory, notifications, sensory themes and large text, Vote & Feedback, and an organizer welcome message. A GetStream preview video call with guest links and screen share is on main.

## 3\. The paradigms

### 3.1 Discord: real-time chat channels

- **Shape:** a server with a sidebar of channels; each text channel is a continuous chat stream; voice channels are always-on rooms people drop into.  
- **Strengths:** feels live and casual; quick back-and-forth; drop-in voice; familiar to many younger users.  
- **Weaknesses:** conversations scroll away; hard to find last week's discussion; can feel noisy and busy; many channels and unread badges; less calm.

### 3.2 Circle-style Spaces (Refuge today)

- **Shape:** topic rooms holding posts; each post has its own comment thread; a combined feed.  
- **Strengths:** calmer; each topic (a prayer request, a week's episode) stays together and findable; good for people who check in a few times a week; fits accessibility and sensory goals.  
- **Weaknesses:** less "live"; quick chatter needs a post first; no drop-in voice.

### 3.3 Slack-style channels with threads

- **Shape:** chat channels like Discord, but replies can branch into threads. (Bema itself runs a BEMA Slack community with channels per session: [https\://www\.bemaevents.com/](https://www.bemaevents.com/))  
- **Strengths:** live chat plus threads to keep topics tidy.  
- **Weaknesses:** threads are easy to miss; workplace feel.

### 3.4 Group-chat apps (WhatsApp, GroupMe, Signal)

- **Shape:** one chat per group, mobile-first, push notifications.  
- **Strengths:** simplest possible; everyone already knows it; fast for reminders and prayer requests.  
- **Weaknesses:** everything in one stream; no structure for episodes or resources.

### 3.5 Forum / message board (Discourse-style)

- **Shape:** categories with long-lived topics.  
- **Strengths:** best for searchable, lasting discussion.  
- **Weaknesses:** feels slow and formal for a small group.

### 3.6 Rhythm-first / course or reading-plan apps (e.g. YouVersion plans, cohort courses)

- **Shape:** content is organized by day or week; you see "this week" first. (The BEMA Liturgy is also available as YouVersion reading plans.)  
- **Strengths:** matches a weekly listen-then-discuss rhythm; tells members what to do next.  
- **Weaknesses:** needs planning tools; less open-ended conversation.

## 4\. Comparison

| Dimension | Discord | Refuge Spaces (Circle-style) | Slack-style | Group chat | Forum | Rhythm-first |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Feel | Live, busy | Calm, organized | Live, tidy | Simple, live | Slow, lasting | Guided |
| Finding last week's discussion | Hard | Easy (post per topic) | Medium | Hard | Easy | Easy (by week) |
| Quick chatter | Excellent | Fair | Good | Excellent | Poor | Fair |
| Prayer requests | Scroll away | Each request is a post with "praying" reactions | Threads | Scroll away | Topics | Could attach to the week |
| Weekly call | Always-on voice room | Call joined by link (a preview link today; a stable weekly link is planned) | Huddles | External link | External link | Call is part of "this week" |
| Sensory load | High | Low | Medium | Medium | Low | Low |
| Fit for Bema weekly group | Good | Good | Good | Fair | Fair | Very good (if built) |

**Open question:** these ratings are judgment calls; the reviewer should challenge them.

## 5\. What Refuge might borrow

1. **From rhythm-first apps:** a "This Week" home that shows the episode(s), listen-by date, call link, and the week's discussion post. This is likely Refuge's biggest differentiator for Bema groups.  
2. **From Discord:** a lighter, faster reply experience in a "General Chat" Space (live updates, quick replies without opening a post). **Open question:** chat-style Space type, or just faster threaded comments?  
3. **From Discord:** a persistent "Weekly Call" room with a stable link rather than drop-in voice. Always-on voice rooms are probably not needed for a weekly group. **Open question:** confirm with Carina.  
4. **From group chat:** gentle push reminders for the call and listening.  
5. **From Slack/forums:** keep threading per topic so prayer requests and episode discussions stay findable.

## 6\. Recommendation for review

Keep Refuge's calm, Circle-style Spaces as the core (they already map to Discord text channels), add a **rhythm-first "This Week" layer** on top, and borrow Discord's **live feel only where chatter happens** (General Chat) plus a **stable weekly call room**. Don't copy always-on voice channels unless Carina's group actually uses them.
