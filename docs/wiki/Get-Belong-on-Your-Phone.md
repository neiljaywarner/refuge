# Get Belong on your phone

A calm page for someone who has never heard of Firebase, a beta, or a home-screen website.

The same words are inside Belong: open **Spaces**, then **Links**, then **Download the Android App** or **Download the iOS App**.

---

## A short version

Belong already works in the browser at [belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://belongonthespectrum.web.app).

The **Android phone app** is the nicer everyday choice. GIF search there can include stickers, clips, and emoji. Message alerts are more reliable there too. The website can search GIFs, but the pictures stay still.

The **iPhone app is not ready**. iPhone members keep using the website until it is.

A few words, in case they are new:

- **Firebase** is the Google page that hands you the Android app before the Play Store. You do not create a Firebase account. You do not need to learn Firebase.
- A **beta** means the app works, and we are still polishing it. A button might move. That is expected.
- A **home-screen website** (people sometimes say PWA) looks like an app, but it is still the website.

## Android app (ready)

This is the one to use day to day.

On your **Android phone**, open [the Android beta page](https://appdistribution.firebase.google.com). A computer cannot install the app onto the phone for you.

1. Sign in with a Google account if it asks. A Gmail address is fine.
2. When you see **Belong on the Spectrum**, tap **Download**.
3. Google may also offer an app called **Firebase App Tester**. That app is Google's. It keeps this download in one place. It is optional.
4. The phone may say it cannot install unknown apps. That warning is normal, because Belong is not in the Play Store yet. Tap **Settings** on that message, then turn on **Allow from this source**. That allows only the app you are using right now, usually Chrome or Firebase App Tester. It does not open installs for the whole phone.
5. Go back and tap **Install**. Belong shows up with your other apps.
6. When you are finished, you can turn **Allow from this source** off again. Google's own steps are on [Download apps to your Pixel phone](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7391672?hl=en). The same idea is under Settings, Apps, Special app access, Install unknown apps, on other Android phones. [Firebase's tester steps](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-distribution/get-set-up-as-a-tester?platform=android) and [Firebase's note if the phone blocks the install](https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-distribution/troubleshooting?platform=android) say the same thing.

Leave **Google Play Protect** on. It may say it does not recognize Belong yet. If the warning says the app is **harmful**, stop and do not install it. [Google's Play Protect page](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2812853?hl=en) explains what that check is. Do not turn Play Protect off for the whole phone.

If the page says you have no apps yet, the open invite is still catching up. Use the [Android website](#android-website-for-testing) steps for today, and tell an organizer.

## iPhone and iPad website

This is how you use Belong on an iPhone today.

Use **Safari**. Chrome on iPhone cannot add Belong the same way.

1. Open Safari and go to [belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://belongonthespectrum.web.app).
2. Tap **Share**. It is a square with an arrow. On iPhone it is usually at the bottom. On iPad it may be at the top.
3. Scroll the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you do not see it, scroll to the bottom, tap **Edit Actions**, and add it.
4. Turn on **Open as Web App** if you see that switch. The name should say **Belong on the Spectrum**. Tap **Add**.
5. Tap the Belong icon on your Home Screen. It opens without Safari's address bar.

GIF search on the website is simpler: still pictures, without stickers, clips, or emoji. Those are in the phone apps. Message alerts are also more reliable once the iPhone app exists. Until TestFlight and the App Store open, this Home Screen icon is the way to use Belong.

Apple's steps: [Turn a website into an app in Safari on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios).

## Android website (for testing)

You only need this if you are testing the website. The [Android app](#android-app-ready) is fine for everyday use, and GIF search and message alerts work better there.

1. Open **Chrome** and go to [belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://belongonthespectrum.web.app).
2. Tap the **three dots** at the top.
3. Tap **Install app**, or **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.
4. The Belong icon appears on your Home Screen and in the app list. It is still the website.

Chrome's steps: [Use web apps](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).

Pictures of the Android website install are on [Why we are not in the app stores yet](Why-No-App-Store-App) and [Android home-screen pictures](PWA-Installation-and-Mobile-Web-Guide).
