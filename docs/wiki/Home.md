# Welcome to Belong on the Spectrum

**Belong on the Spectrum** is a calm, sensory-safe online community for autistic and neurodivergent adults, friends, families, and the people who care with them. We partner with [DFW Church](https://dfwchurch.org).

**Open Belong:** [belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://belongonthespectrum.web.app/)  
**Try newer work:** [dev-belongonthespectrum.web.app](https://dev-belongonthespectrum.web.app/)  
**Our website:** [belongonthespectrum.com](https://www.belongonthespectrum.com/)

[onthespectrum.web.app](https://onthespectrum.web.app/) is not a Belong site.

### Three easy ways to help

1. **Open Belong** and tap **Vote & Feedback**. Then tap **Vote on Features** and press the thumb next to what matters to you.
2. **Share feedback** in that same place. The choices say what they mean: **Works Great**, **Idea / Tweak**, or **Bug / Confusing**.
3. **Say hello** in **Start Here** so the rooms feel warm, not empty.

## What Belong tries to feel like

- You can choose a calmer look: **Clean Slate**, **Sage Botanical**, **Monastic Dark**, or **High Contrast**. The menu shortens these to **Clean**, **Sage**, **Dark**, and **Contrast**.
- When you write, you can say what you need up front. **Comfort Only** means company, not advice.
- Care responses are words such as **Praying** and **Support**. They are not a popularity score.
