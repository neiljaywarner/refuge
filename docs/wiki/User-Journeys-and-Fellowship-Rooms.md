# 🧭 User Journeys & Fellowship Rooms

Sanctuary organizes community life into distinct, peaceful rooms rather than a single chaotic timeline.

---

## 🏛️ The Four Fellowship Spaces

1. **🙏 #prayer-sanctuary**
   - The heart of community intercession.
   - Members post personal prayers and burdens.
   - Typical tags: `[Prayer Request]`, `[Comfort Only]`.
   - Interaction: Other members tap `🙏 Praying` to silently affirm that they are lifting the request in prayer.

2. **📖 #weekly-study**
   - Spiritual growth and scripture reflection.
   - Members discuss weekly passages without hurry.
   - Typical tags: `[Question]`, `[Resource]`.

3. **📢 #announcements**
   - Official community notices and fellowship updates.
   - Read-only or leader-curated notifications presented peacefully without intrusive popups.

4. **☕ #general-fellowship**
   - Casual conversation, shared joys, gratitude, and informal encouragement.

---

## 🏷️ Intent Tags & Conversational Boundaries

Every post requires an Intent Tag before submission:
- **`[Prayer Request]`**: Seeking communal intercession.
- **`[Comfort Only]`**: Sharing a vulnerability; signifies that the author seeks empathy and quiet presence rather than analysis or advice.
- **`[Question]`**: Seeking thoughtful biblical or life insights.
- **`[Resource]`**: Sharing a devotion, article, podcast, or hymn.
