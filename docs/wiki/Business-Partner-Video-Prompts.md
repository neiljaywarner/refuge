# 🎬 Gemini Web Video Prompts (Business Partner Overview)

> **Companion to**: **[Business & Partnership Overview](Business-Partner-Overview)**  
> **Goal**: Copy-paste prompts to generate a warm, non-technical overview video for a prospective business partner or advisor using **Gemini on the Web** ([gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)) or **Google Vids** ([vids.new](https://vids.new)).

---

## Option A: Direct in [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) (**Tools → Video**)

Gemini's built-in **Video** button creates an 8-second HD clip **with synchronized spoken voiceover and calm background audio**. Leave quotation marks *off* the spoken voiceover line as written below so no floating subtitle marks appear on screen.

### 1. Single One-Paste Video Prompt (8-Second Warm Hook)
```text
Warm cinematic 16:9 shot inside a sunlit, quiet coffee shop with natural wood tables and soft greenery. Two longtime friends sit comfortably across from each other with mugs of coffee, smiling in relaxed conversation while looking at a clean, calm community page on a laptop screen. Slow, steady camera push-in, shallow depth of field, warm morning light, 35mm editorial documentary feel, peaceful and unhurried atmosphere, no on-screen text overlays. Warm, friendly male voiceover says: Most online communities are noisy, overwhelming, and cost churches over a thousand dollars a year. So we built a calm digital home that starts as real ministry and grows into a simple, sustainable business. Audio: gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar, quiet warm cafe ambiance, clear natural voiceover.
```

### 2. Optional 3-Clip Story Sequence (~24 Seconds Total)

- **Clip 1 — Pick a Fight (The Problem)**:
  ```text
  Warm editorial 16:9 medium shot of a thoughtful church organizer sitting at a tidy wooden desk at dusk, gently closing a cluttered, stressful tablet full of red notification bells and setting aside an expensive annual invoice, exhaling with relief. Soft warm desk lamp lighting, shallow depth of field, calm documentary style, no floating text. Warm male voiceover says: Churches and support groups are stuck between noisy social media and thousand-dollar community tools nobody enjoys using. Audio: soft ambient room tone transitioning into a single warm piano chord, clear natural voiceover.
  ```
- **Clip 2 — Half, Not Half-Assed (The Calm Solution)**:
  ```text
  Sunlit 16:9 medium close-up of an adult woman in a cozy sage sweater sipping tea peacefully at a kitchen table, smiling gently at a calm, uncluttered community screen on her laptop in warm morning sunlight. Smooth slow camera pan, natural film grain, serene mood, no on-screen text overlays. Warm male voiceover says: Belong on the Spectrum strips away the noise and keeps only what matters—calm rooms where neurodivergent adults, families, and churches actually belong. Audio: gentle acoustic guitar melody, peaceful morning room ambiance, clear natural voiceover.
  ```
- **Clip 3 — Embrace Constraints & Zero-Pressure Partnership**:
  ```text
  Warm 16:9 shot of two friends who trust each other sharing coffee at an outdoor wooden patio table under soft afternoon sunlight, nodding thoughtfully over a simple notebook sketch with genuine warmth and zero rush. Gentle steadicam movement, natural golden-hour light, authentic documentary realism, no floating text. Warm male voiceover says: With near-zero monthly overhead, we can serve thousands of churches and non-profits sustainably. Zero pressure—help however you want, run with the business, or just share your wisdom as a friend. Audio: warm acoustic guitar resolution, light outdoor breeze, clear friendly voiceover.
  ```

## Option B: Full 60-Second Narrated Video in Google Vids ([vids.new](https://vids.new) → *"Help me create"*)

For a complete **60-second multi-scene narrated video** with slides, calm B-roll, voiceover, and gentle music in one click, open [vids.new](https://vids.new), select **Help me create**, and paste:

```text
Create a warm, grounded, 60-second business overview video for a trusted friend and experienced business consultant introducing "Belong on the Spectrum" (belongonthespectrum.com).

IMPORTANT TONE & RULES:
- Zero technical jargon: do NOT mention programming languages, frameworks, servers, or software buzzwords.
- Tone: Sincere, unhurried, 37signals / REWORK style—practical, humble, high-trust, and zero pressure.
- Visual style: Warm cream, sage green, and deep slate palette; calm photography of real people, quiet coffee tables, churches, and families; gentle transitions; soft acoustic background music.

STRUCTURE (5 SCENES, ~12 SECONDS EACH):
1. The Real-World Problem: Churches, autism non-profits, and support groups want a warm weekday home, but Facebook and Discord are noisy and overwhelming, while Circle and legacy church suites charge $1,000 to $10,000 a year for cluttered features nobody touches.
2. Live Ministry Proof: Before our own church's $1,500/year Circle contract ends on October 31, we built Belong on the Spectrum—a quiet, ad-free, sensory-comfortable home where real members are moving in right now.
3. Built the 37signals Way:
   - Pick a Fight: Calm human belonging instead of attention traps and App Store gatekeeping.
   - Half, Not Half-Assed: Focused weekly rooms, gentle care responses, and built-in member voting done remarkably well.
   - Embrace Constraints: Near-$0 monthly baseline cost, meaning zero burn pressure and immediate healthy margins.
4. The Business Horizon: What serves our home church for free solves the exact same problem for thousands of churches, disability ministries, and recovery groups—at $49–$99/month for hosted spaces plus $1,500–$5,000 for white-glove setup and community migration.
5. An Open-Handed Invitation: Friendship comes first with zero pressure. Whether you want to offer occasional advice over coffee, bring it to consulting clients, co-build, or take the business reins and run with it (keeping its calm heart, shared founder credit, and fair financial participation), I would love to hear what you see.
```
