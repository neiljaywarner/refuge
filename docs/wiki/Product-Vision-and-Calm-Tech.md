# 🕊️ Product Vision & The Calm-Tech Standard

## The Problem
Modern digital communication platforms are engineered for hyper-stimulation and urgency:
- Numerical red badges trigger subconscious anxiety.
- Typing bubbles ("... is typing") demand immediate attention.
- Algorithmic feeds prioritize outrage and controversy to drive engagement metrics.
- Binary "Likes" transform authentic sharing into a popularity contest.

## The Sanctuary Solution
**Sanctuary** is a digital refuge designed for spiritual rest, contemplative fellowship, and genuine mutual support.

```
Addictive Social Media                 Sanctuary Calm-Tech Standard
───────────────────────                ────────────────────────────
🔴 Red unread badge counters     ──>   🚫 Zero badges anywhere
💬 Typing bubbles & read receipts ──>   🚫 Asynchronous reflection
📈 Algorithmic outrage feed       ──>   ⏳ Strict chronological flow
👍 Performative "Likes"           ──>   🙏 Qualitative care affirmations
🌈 Glitzy, bright animations      ──>   🌿 4 soothing sensory modes
```

## The Five Calm-Tech Pillars
1. **Zero Badges**: The app never demands your attention. You check it when you have quiet time, not when a push notification forces you.
2. **Zero Urgency**: Nobody knows if you are typing or if you have read a message. Take minutes, hours, or days to respond.
3. **Intent Tags**: Readers know emotional expectations upfront. If a post is tagged `[Comfort Only]`, members know not to offer unsolicited advice.
4. **Qualitative Care**: Clicking `🙏 6 Praying` or `🤍 4 Support` affirms the author without algorithmic gamification.
5. **Sensory Ergonomics**: Four curated palettes protect eyes and nervous systems: **Sage** (organic tranquility), **Clean** (neutral day), **Charcoal** (evening), and **Contrast** (WCAG AAA accessibility).
