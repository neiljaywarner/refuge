Feature: Compact Mobile Reflection Composer (#447)
  As a Belong community member on a mobile device
  I want the feed composer to occupy minimal vertical space by default
  So that I can read existing reflections and community discussions without them being crowded out

  Scenario: Inline reflection composer appears compact on feed load
    Given the member is viewing any fellowship space feed
    Then they should see a slim single-line reflection trigger bar
    And the bar contains "Share an unhurried thought..."
    And the bar contains an "Add Meme" shortcut button and "Expand" button
    And the full card with "Intent Tag" dropdown and large multiline input is hidden

  Scenario: Expanding the inline composer reveals full options
    Given the member is looking at the compact reflection bar
    When they tap the expand button or begin typing a thought
    Then the composer smoothly expands in place
    And they see the "Intent Tag" dropdown
    And they see the "Add Meme / GIF" button
    And they see the "Publish Reflection" button
    And they see the "Collapse" icon button

  Scenario: Publishing collapses the inline composer back to compact
    Given the member has entered an unhurried thought in the expanded composer
    When they tap "Publish Reflection"
    Then the reflection is posted to the active fellowship space
    And the composer collapses back to the calm, compact single-line bar
