Feature: Multi-User Concurrency & Real-Time Sync in Christian Fellowship
  As neurodivergent Christian disciples (Alice in Dallas & Bob in Fort Worth)
  We want to share prayer reflections and encourage one another in real time
  So that our fellowship remains calm, synchronized, and free of race conditions or lost updates.

  Background:
    Given the Belong on the Spectrum shared reactive comment and reflection store is active
    And Alice is signed in representing "Dallas Church of Christ"
    And Bob is signed in representing "Fort Worth Church of Christ"
    And both disciples are viewing the discussion for reflection "1" (David M. prayer request)

  Scenario: Real-Time Stream Synchronization Across Concurrent Disciples
    When Alice posts a reflection comment "Praying for gentle rhythms today, brother." with tag "[Prayer]"
    Then Bob's active discussion view receives Alice's comment in real time without refreshing
    And Alice's church badge and name "Alice M. (Dallas)" are visible to Bob

  Scenario: Nested Reply Propagation Under Concurrent Multi-Client Browsing
    Given Bob sees Alice's reflection comment in his live stream
    When Bob posts a nested reply "Amen Alice! Psalm 23:2 brings so much comfort." to Alice's comment
    Then Alice's active discussion view immediately renders Bob's nested reply with indicator "↳"
    And both disciples see the total reply count incremented synchronously

  Scenario: Concurrent Reaction Tally Reconciliation Without Lost Updates
    When Alice adds a "pray" reaction to a comment simultaneously as Bob adds a "peace" reaction
    Then the comment repository registers both reactions atomically
    And both Alice's view and Bob's view reflect accurate reaction tallies
    And no race condition or overwrite occurs between the concurrent disciples
