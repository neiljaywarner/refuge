Feature: Multi-User Concurrency & Real-Time Sync in Christian Fellowship
  As neurodivergent Christian disciples (Disciple Alpha & Disciple Beta)
  We want to share prayer reflections and encourage one another in real time
  So that our fellowship remains calm, synchronized, and free of race conditions or lost updates.

  Background:
    Given the Belong on the Spectrum shared reactive comment and reflection store is active
    And Disciple Alpha is signed in representing a Dallas fellowship
    And Disciple Beta is signed in representing a Fort Worth fellowship
    And both disciples are viewing the discussion for reflection "1" (David M. prayer request)

  Scenario: Real-Time Stream Synchronization Across Concurrent Disciples
    When Disciple Alpha posts a reflection comment "Praying for gentle rhythms today, brother." with tag "[Prayer]"
    Then Disciple Beta's active discussion view receives the comment in real time without refreshing
    And Disciple Alpha's name "Disciple Alpha (Dallas)" is visible to Disciple Beta

  Scenario: Nested Reply Propagation Under Concurrent Multi-Client Browsing
    Given Disciple Beta sees Disciple Alpha's reflection comment in the live stream
    When Disciple Beta posts a nested reply "Amen! Psalm 23:2 brings so much comfort." to Disciple Alpha's comment
    Then Disciple Alpha's active discussion view immediately renders the nested reply with indicator "↳"
    And both disciples see the total reply count incremented synchronously

  Scenario: Concurrent Reaction Tally Reconciliation Without Lost Updates
    When Disciple Alpha adds a "pray" reaction to a comment simultaneously as Disciple Beta adds a "peace" reaction
    Then the comment repository registers both reactions atomically
    And both disciples' views reflect accurate reaction tallies
    And no race condition or overwrite occurs between the concurrent disciples
