Feature: Profile Persistence and ICOC Church Dropdown Selection (#448)
  As a Belong community member
  I want my preferred display name and ICOC church selection to persist across browser refreshes and app reboots
  And I want an accessible dropdown selector for my church congregation rather than tapping buttons

  Scenario: Member selects church congregation via dropdown and saves profile
    Given the member opens the Sign-In / Member Profile dialog
    When they enter their preferred display name
    And they open the ICOC church affiliation dropdown
    And they select their congregation from the list
    And they tap "Save & Sign In"
    Then their profile is saved with the chosen display name and church
    And the session is persisted to local and cloud storage

  Scenario: Member profile metadata persists across app restarts and page reloads
    Given a member previously signed in with a preferred display name and ICOC church
    When the member refreshes the web browser or reboots the mobile app
    Then the app restores their preferred display name without reverting to guest defaults
    And their ICOC church affiliation is immediately displayed

  Scenario: Signing out clears persistent storage
    Given a signed-in member with persisted profile metadata
    When they open the profile dialog and tap "Sign Out"
    Then the local profile storage is cleared
    And the app reverts to the peaceful guest member state
