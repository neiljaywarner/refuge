# Full Calm Notifications BDD Specification (Issue #442)
# Driver: Marionette MCP / CLI / PatrolWidgetTest
# Neurodivergent Target: Calm, unhurried notifications with @ mentions, DMs, shade, and badge support.

Feature: Full Calm Notifications Feature
  As an autistic member of Belong on the Spectrum
  I want an unhurried, sensory-friendly notifications hub with @ mentions and direct messages
  And I want OS badges and notifications delivered silently to the shade
  So that I stay informed without sensory panic or urgency red dots

  Scenario: Unread notification badge on bottom navigation bell
    Given I launch Belong on the Spectrum
    Then I see the bottom navigation bar
    And the "Notifications" bell tab displays an unread count badge
    And the badge uses calm forest green styling instead of bright red

  Scenario: Browsing and filtering notification history
    Given I tap the "Notifications" tab in the bottom bar
    Then I see the "Notifications" view
    And I see the calm delivery banner
    When I tap the "Mentions" filter chip
    Then I see only notifications where other members mentioned me with @
    When I tap the "Direct Messages" filter chip
    Then I see only notifications for private 1-on-1 messages

  Scenario: Marking all notifications as read
    Given I am on the "Notifications" view with unread items
    When I tap the "Mark all read" button
    Then all unread indicators are removed
    And the bottom navigation bell badge disappears
    And the OS launcher icon badge is cleared

  Scenario: Testing OS Notification Shade delivery
    Given I am on the "Notifications" view
    When I open "Calm Notification Preferences"
    And I tap "Preview shade notification (debug)"
    Then a silent notification is delivered to the Android notification shade
    And pulling down the shade displays "Belong" without triggering sound or vibration
