## 📋 Teleprompter Script (Clean Continuous Text)

If you use a teleprompter app or read directly from your phone/screen:

```text
For the past year, the Belong on the Spectrum ministry—a global fellowship of autistic and neurodivergent Christians—relied on a proprietary portal that cost over fifteen hundred dollars a year.

Worse than the cost was the sensory noise: red notification badges, urgency loops, and algorithmic feeds that overwhelmed members who simply wanted a quiet place to pray and connect.

Tonight, we replaced that expensive, anxious SaaS stack with Sanctuary: an unhurried, calm-tech fellowship platform built full-stack on Flutter and Serverpod 4.

Under the hood, Serverpod 4 powers everything. With typed SPI YAML models, our client and server share one unified Dart language. We get end-to-end type safety, migrations on PostgreSQL 16, and real-time WebSocket streams with zero JSON boilerplate.

Instead of spamming client push notifications, our Serverpod Cloud backend computes a peaceful Quiet Digest—summarizing community blessings and prayer burdens in a gentle, aggregate banner.

Sanctuary honors cognitive load. Members can instantly switch between four calm sensory themes: botanical Sage, daylight Clean Slate, nocturnal Monastic Dark, and AAA High Contrast. There are no red badges or urgency traps anywhere.

Humor and special interests are essential to fellowship. Our built-in calm-tech meme gallery offers wholesome, curated neurodivergent humor without strobe animations or third-party tracking. We tap Elijah Under the Broom Tree, and post with qualitative care tags.

To prove real-world durability, here is Sanctuary running simultaneously on two physical Google Pixel 9a devices communicating live over T-Mobile LTE cellular data straight to our Serverpod Cloud instance.

Sanctuary is engineered for mission-critical reliability. If internet drops during a quiet retreat, our resilient repository automatically falls back to offline mode within 800 milliseconds without interrupting the user. Twenty-one automated tests verify every interaction.

Sanctuary proves that modern communities can replace expensive, anxiety-inducing platforms with fast, calm, full-stack Dart software. Huge thanks to BuilderBase and the Serverpod team. Thank you, and may you find stillness.
```

## 🎧 Audio Generation Command

To re-generate or adjust the voice narration speed on macOS:

```bash
# Recommended: Natural conversational pacing (165 wpm)
say -v Samantha -r 165 -f docs/submission_packet/voiceover_narration.txt -o sanctuary_demo_video_narration.m4a

# British alternative: Daniel
say -v Daniel -r 160 -f docs/submission_packet/voiceover_narration.txt -o sanctuary_demo_video_narration_daniel.m4a
```
